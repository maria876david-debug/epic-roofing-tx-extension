# How Long Does a Roof Replacement Take in Houston?

Homeowners usually ask about cost first. The second question — how long will my home be a construction site — matters just as much.

A typical Houston replacement runs like this:

- **Inspection and estimate** — the roof is measured and inspected; materials and scope are agreed.
- **Material delivery** — shingles and underlayment arrive before tear-off day.
- **Tear-off and installation** — many single-family homes finish in 1–3 days, depending on size, pitch and decking condition.
- **Cleanup and final check** — debris removal, magnet sweep for nails, and a walkthrough.

Weather is the wild card: Gulf rain can pause a job mid-way, and a good schedule plans around it.
