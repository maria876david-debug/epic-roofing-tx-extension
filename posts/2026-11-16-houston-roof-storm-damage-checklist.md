# After a Houston Storm: A Roof Checklist Before You Call Anyone

After a Houston storm, check your roof before you call anyone - from the ground, never by climbing up. Look for missing or lifted shingles, granules collecting in the gutters, bent flashing around chimneys and vents, and any water stains on ceilings or in the attic. Take dated photos of everything you find; they matter for insurance.

If you see a sagging section, a large hole, or water pouring in, that is an emergency - move valuables, catch the water, and call for help right away.
