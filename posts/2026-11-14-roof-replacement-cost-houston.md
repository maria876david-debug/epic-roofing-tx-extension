# How Much Does a Roof Replacement Cost in Houston?

There is no single price for a roof replacement in Houston. The cost depends on the size of the roof, the material chosen, the pitch, and whether old roofing has to be removed first. Permits and local labour rates play a part too.

Our new guide walks through each of these factors so homeowners can see what drives the price up — and what questions to ask a roofer before signing anything.
