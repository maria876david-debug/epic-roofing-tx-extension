# How Long Does a Houston Roof Really Last? What Shortens Its Life

A Houston roof does not fail on a calendar date - it fails when heat, storms and small problems add up. The two biggest life-shorteners are the Houston sun, which dries and cracks shingles year after year, and storm damage that goes unnoticed: a lifted shingle after one storm becomes a leak in the next. Poor attic ventilation makes it worse, trapping heat under the roof and ageing the material from below. The good news is that most of this is visible from the ground if you look: missing granules in the gutters, curled or cracked shingles, and flashing that has pulled away from a chimney.
