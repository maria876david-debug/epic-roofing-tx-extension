# Roof Repair or Replacement in Houston? Start With What Each One Costs

Every Houston homeowner with a leaking or ageing roof faces the same fork in the road: repair it, or replace it? The honest answer starts with numbers, because the two options live in completely different price worlds — and choosing wrong in either direction wastes money.

## What a repair costs — and what it can fix

Minor roof repairs in Houston typically run $300–$600: a few lifted or missing shingles, a failed pipe boot, a small flashing leak around a vent or chimney. A repair is the right call when the damage is local, the rest of the roof is sound, and the roof still has years of life in it. Spending a few hundred dollars to fix one leak on a healthy ten-year-old roof is good sense.

## What a replacement costs — and when it is the only real answer

Most roof replacement projects in Houston run $6,712–$13,123, with an average around $9,752. That is serious money, which is why nobody should spend it a day before they need to — or a day after. Replacement becomes the right answer when repairs stop holding: leaks appearing in different places, shingles losing their granules across whole slopes, a roof at or past the end of its expected life, or storm damage spread across the roof rather than in one spot. At that point each $300–$600 repair is buying months, not years, and the repair bills start adding up toward the replacement you were avoiding.

## The middle ground most people miss

Between "one small repair" and "full replacement" sits the inspection. A proper roof inspection — on the roof, not from the driveway — tells you which side of the line you are actually on: how much life is left, where the weak points are, and whether a repair done now genuinely extends the roof or just delays the bill. Guessing from the ground is how homeowners end up paying for three repairs and then the replacement anyway.
