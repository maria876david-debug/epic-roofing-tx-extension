# What a Professional Roof Inspection Covers (and Why It Beats a Look From the Ground)

From the ground, a roof can look fine while it is quietly failing. A professional inspection goes where your eyes cannot: up on the roof surface, checking shingles for lifting, cracking and lost granules; along the flashing around chimneys, vents and valleys, where most leaks actually start; and inside the attic, where water stains, damp insulation and daylight through the decking tell the truth about leaks that have not reached a ceiling yet. You get photos, a written condition report and a straight answer on what needs fixing now, what can wait, and what it will cost - which is also the paperwork an insurance claim wants after a storm.
