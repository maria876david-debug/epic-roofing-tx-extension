# Roofing Warranties in Houston: Workmanship vs Manufacturer — What Each One Covers

Two warranties come with a new roof in Houston, and they cover two completely different things. Homeowners who mix them up discover the difference only when something goes wrong — which is the worst possible time to learn it.

## The manufacturer's warranty: the materials

The manufacturer warranties the roofing materials — the shingles, underlayment and flashing it made. If a shingle fails because of a manufacturing defect, the manufacturer covers it, usually for a long period. What it does not cover is how the roof was installed. A perfectly good shingle fitted badly is not the manufacturer's problem, and a manufacturer can refuse a claim if the installation didn't follow its instructions.

## The workmanship warranty: the installation

The workmanship warranty comes from the roofer who installed the roof, and it covers installation mistakes — flashing fitted wrong, nails in the wrong place, a valley that channels water where it shouldn't. This is the warranty that matters most in Houston, because our heat, humidity and storm seasons punish installation errors fast. A leak in the first few years of a roof's life is almost always an installation problem, not a materials problem.

## The questions to ask before you sign

**How long is each warranty, in writing?** Get both periods stated in the contract, not in a conversation. Workmanship periods vary widely between roofers; a longer workmanship warranty is one of the clearest signs a roofer trusts its own crews.

**What voids the warranty?** Common exclusions include another contractor working on the roof, unapproved modifications, and storm damage — storms are an insurance matter, not a warranty matter, and your roofer should say so plainly.

**Who do you call when something leaks?** If the answer depends on whether the problem is materials or installation, you need a roofer who will come and look first, not one who sends you to the manufacturer on day one.

## The Houston factor

Houston roofs work harder than roofs in most of the country: summer heat bakes shingles, Gulf humidity feeds growth under them, and storm seasons test every seam. A warranty that sounds generous on paper is only worth the company standing behind it. Choose the roofer, then read the warranty — not the other way round.
